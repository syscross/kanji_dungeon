# KANJI DUNGEON 【漢字地獄】

An endless dark-fantasy AI TRPG & Kanji card roguelike spanning **6,355 Kanji** (Kanken Level 10 to Level 1 / JLPT N5 to N1+).

- **Play Live:** [https://kanji.syscross.dev](https://kanji.syscross.dev)
- **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key):** Supports Google Gemini (Free Tier), Anthropic Claude (`claude-haiku-5-5`), and OpenRouter directly in your browser via `localStorage`.
